The challenge

In health & beauty, shoppers spread their purchases across many stores. The chain wanted its existing customers to bring more of their visits, and more of their spend, to its stores.

The campaign

Every purchase earned progress toward a porcelain dinnerware collection. Over 17 weeks, shoppers came back to complete their set, piece by piece.

The results

Shoppers who redeemed went from 2.2 to 2.8 visits per week, a 26.9% increase, and the chain’s total sales grew 7.9%. In total, 629,000 pieces were redeemed.

Why it worked

Visit frequency compounds. Each extra trip is another basket, and the habit built over 17 weeks keeps shoppers coming back to the chain instead of its competitors.
